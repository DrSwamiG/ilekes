Cowrie - 1

4 Cowrie Shell Divination

Cowrie -1: is an introductory divination module that introduces Aborisha Initiates to 4 Cowrie Shell Divination System over a five(5) week period. Qualified participants are awarded Certificates of Completion as Novice Cowrie Diviners.
This module is a 35-day period to extend the recent initiation energy, love, correspondence and support for Aborisha. After initiation, one is required to re-initiate daily. This daily initiation provides the energy needed to overcome the resistance of attraction towards the lower self.
For the duration of the module, initiates will be required to:

  • Submit for review daily responses to reflection questions, designed to support and instill conscious choices,
  • Submit for review daily pictures of 4 cowrie castings for the daily review/comment,
  • Share at Weekly Zoom.
